## Context

Statistics Canada publishes journey-to-work mode shares for the Edmonton census metropolitan area and the city (2016 and 2021 censuses), and the City's Household Travel Survey publishes all-trip mode shares. The claim is the commuting one; the "2.3%" and "proportional" forms are recorded as the same argument in other units.
