## Context

The claim that the City signed an agreement to make Edmonton a "15 minute city". The City Plan (2020) adopts "15-minute districts" as a planning concept; whether the City signed any external agreement (a network pledge, a charter, a C40-type commitment) is a matter of record. Related to the published fifteen-minute-districts story, which tested whether district plans restrict where residents can travel (Contradicted); this is a different proposition, about the existence of an agreement.
