# Intake record: the two published infill-prices claims, read again under triage

Recorded 2026-09-02 by Stew. Purpose: methodology v1.13 says a story
leaves the findings board when every claim on it is Not established
because the public record cannot answer the question people ask. The
test is a triage read of the published claims, so the call is not the
editor's alone. This record asks for that read.

## Claim 1 (published as ip-teardown-price-gap, Not established, unanimous)

Circulating form, supplied by the founder from an Edmonton infill
thread, source not captured:

> "AFFORDABLE!?! Have you looked at the prices of these houses?! A
> $350,000 house is torn down and a $1,000,000 house goes up in its
> place!!"

Proposition as checked (methodology v1.10 magnitude re-run): on
Edmonton lots where a house was demolished for infill, the housing
built in its place is typically worth about three times the house that
came down. The panel found Edmonton publishes no series joining a
demolished house to its replacement; one reviewer hand-joined eight
lots (ratios about 1.4 to 3.9) and called it an anecdote.

## Claim 2 (published as ip-infill-affordable, Not established, split)

Proposition: new infill housing in Edmonton is affordable relative to
the existing housing it replaces or to citywide prices. Published
new-home prices do not identify infill by dwelling form.

## What the read is for

answered and did not, or because no public record can answer the
question at the level people ask it? If the second, the story leaves
the findings board and is listed on /considered as "Checked; the
