## Context

Edmonton publishes bicycle counter data (the Daily Pedestrian and Bike
Counts dataset on the open data portal, and counter locations on its
bike maps page), reported December 2023 winter counts in its own
communications, and evaluated the Downtown Bike Network's use in 2018.
The City's 2023-2026 Active Transportation Network Expansion Program
opened about 35 km of routes in 2024 and 2025, so the question can be
asked about the new routes as well as the older network.
