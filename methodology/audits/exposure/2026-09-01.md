## 2. Warn dispositions

**PII — 8 findings, all in `evidence/files/YF-EV-0030-1384`, lines 59–79 and
1496–1498. ACCEPTED, permanently.**

Six academic corresponding-author email addresses (redacted here as
`<author>@uqam.ca`, `@umontreal.ca`, `@umontpellier.fr`, `@uth.tmc.edu`), each
appearing in a `citation_author_email` meta tag and, for one author, again in a
`schema.org/Person` `itemprop="email"` block. These are the journal's own
published contact addresses for the authors of the archived article, on an
open-access CC BY 4.0 page — public professional contact information that the
authors deliberately published, not private personal data.

No redaction, for two reasons. Editing the archived bytes would break the
`sha256` recorded in `evidence/registry/YF-EV-0030.yaml`, destroying the
integrity guarantee that is the entire purpose of holding an archive. And the
archive is a faithful copy of a public page; selectively altering it would make
it a worse record without making anything less public.

This is a standing acceptance. Per the process doc, it should not be re-noted in
future records unless the underlying facts change.

**LONG QUOTES — 0 findings.** The longest `quote` field across all committed
review JSONs is well inside the 75-word threshold, consistent with the per-run
release checks already committed under each story's `gate/`.

### Advisory — founder judgement, not defects

All four concern `docs/brainstorm-transcript.md`, the one tracked file that has
never passed through any gate. It holds no secrets, no private individuals and
no infrastructure detail, but it is raw private thinking, and it is already in
published git history — so these are decisions about what to do next, not
redactions this audit can perform.

1. **Business plan in detail** (`:6078-6485`, `:7152-7907`) — monetization
   design, price points, a revenue projection, the commercial spin-out,
   licensing strategy, and tax-credit and accelerator plans including a named
   programme and deadline. `src/pages/support.astro` already discloses the
   for-profit structure; the figures and the funding strategy are not disclosed
   anywhere else.
2. **Origin framing** (`:16-24`, `:750-765`, `:1326`) — the brief names the
   councillor explainer pages that inspired the project and states a policy
   conclusion; the transcript itself pushes back that this is a policy position
   a skeptic could use, and notes the launch set skews toward answering
   objections to City policy. `src/pages/about.astro` discloses the founder's
   views, which blunts this, but the transcript is far more explicit about
   intent than the About page.
3. **Wording risk** (`:566`, `:1011-1025`) — the alias/SEO mechanism is
   described as pages that "secretly" understand hostile search language. The
   mechanism is benign and shipped; the word quotes badly.
4. **Named sitting politicians in launch strategy** (`:16`, `:879-888`) —
   non-disparaging, about public figures in public roles, but written as private
   planning.

Recommended disposition, for the founder to accept or reject: leave the
transcript published (removing it now would not unpublish it, and its presence
is itself a transparency asset), and decide separately whether to add a short
preamble marking it as raw unedited brainstorming that predates the methodology.

### Advisory — process

5. **Untracked working files are one `git add -A` from publication.**

6. **Prompt-injection surface at `scripts/panel/run-reviewer.sh:153-173`.**
