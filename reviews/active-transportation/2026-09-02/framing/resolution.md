Under the cap (methodology v1.12), after the second report the editor
resolves in writing every finding still OPEN, and the third report
confirms or parks. Check 2 (framing/check-2.md) left three OPEN, all
consequences of the reframe. Each is adopted as the checker proposed;
none is argued.

**Check 4, Claim A, the comparison year.** Adopted. The advocacy page
was captured on 2022-12-12 and the 2023 operating budget was approved on
2022-12-16, so the annual snow budget in force when the statement was
made was 2022's. The primary denominator is now the approved 2022 gross
Snow and Ice Control expenditure budget; the final approved 2023 figure
is the required alternative; the ratio and the classification are
computed under both; 2022 actual expenditure is context, because an
actual is not a budget.

**Check 4 and check 7, Claim B, the roads boundary.** Adopted. The
City's adopted 2023-2026 Capital Budget publishes no roads-only total;
its Table 8 line is "Active Pathways and Roads Service", $1,794.0
million, which includes active pathways. The brief now fixes the
roads-only primary by the City's own label: every capital profile whose
sheet in that document carries Service Category "Roads" or
"Neighbourhood Renewal", with a named exception list (pedestrian-only
and parking-technology profiles out; three vehicle-carrying bridge
profiles whose sheets carry no category in), and the editor's reading

the sheets rather than build their own. No mixed profile is
apportioned. The Table 8 line is the required alternative, reported
under its full published name and never called a roads total, with
CM-20-0330 and the other active-pathway funding left visible inside it.
Classification is required under both.
