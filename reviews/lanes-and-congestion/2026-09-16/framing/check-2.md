## 2. Does the proposition test what the post asserts?

Previous Claim 4 finding: **WEAKENED**.

The revision correctly excludes renewal, transit priority, LRT construction and generic safety from “traffic calming.” It still does not test its full normalized proposition.

[framing] Claim 4 states a two-part proposition but makes the second part verdict-irrelevant.

Quoted brief text:

> “its contemporaneous record says the lane would have been removed for traffic calming even without the bike lane, and that adding the bike lane during the work combined projects or saved money.”

but:

> “Whether the document also says the bike lane was combined with the work or saved money is reported per corridor as a qualification, not as a condition.”

A Supported verdict can therefore issue when no record says projects were combined or money was saved. That is not support for the proposition as written. The definition of O also does not require the record to establish the stated counterfactual—“would have been removed … even without the bike lane.”

Replacement wording:

> Let C be qualifying corridors with a contemporaneous reason record; O those where the record expressly says the through-lane removal would have proceeded for traffic calming without the bike lane; K those where the record says the bike lane was added to work already proceeding to combine projects or save money; and J those satisfying both O and K on the same corridor.
>
> - **Supported** if C is at least 3 and J is more than half of C.
> - **Partially supported** if at least one of O or K is non-zero and Supported does not apply.
> - **Contradicted** if C is at least 3, O and K are both zero, and the records affirmatively give incompatible accounts.
> - **Not established** otherwise.
>
> A document merely naming traffic calming as one purpose does not establish that the lane would have been removed without the bike lane.

Claim 5 proxy and removal: **RESOLVED**. Carrying it to the 79 Street question is responsive to both earlier findings.

[framing] Claim 1 substitutes temporal association for the causal assertion.

Quoted brief text:

> “Where the City of Edmonton removed a motor-vehicle through lane to install a bike lane, motor-vehicle congestion on that corridor increased.”

and:

> “The measure is the City’s published before-and-after motor-vehicle travel time or delay.”

The posts say lane removal causes congestion. An uncontrolled before/after increase does not establish that the conversion caused it; other construction, traffic growth, season, signal changes or diversion could produce the difference. The brief itself asks what conversions “did to traffic,” which is causal.

Replacement wording:

> **Normalized proposition:** Removing a motor-vehicle through lane to install a bike lane caused peak-period average motor-vehicle travel time or delay on the converted corridor to rise by at least 10 per cent, with the result at 5 per cent reported as the alternative.
>
> A corridor counts toward U only where the City evaluation expressly attributes the change to the conversion or uses a comparison, counterfactual or other design capable of supporting that attribution. An unadjusted before-and-after difference is reported as a qualification and does not by itself carry the causal verdict.

## 3. Is it the strongest fair reading?

Claim 3 geographic generality: **RESOLVED**. Eight of 15 district-plan areas, with five reported as the alternative, is a recognizable test of “all throughout,” rather than the former two-area rule.

Claim 1 coverage: **WEAKENED**. A coverage requirement was added, but its denominator conflicts with Claim 3 and its primary/alternative operation leaves a gap; see the defects under check 4.

[framing] Claim 2 still lets one local example support a citywide generalization.

Quoted brief text:

> “Supported if S is at least 1 and D is at least 1 and the evidence connects both links in the same evaluated population or network.”

The “evaluated population or network” may be one corridor or a narrow route-user population. Yet the proposition says Edmonton’s infrastructure reduces congestion “in the city,” and the stakes correctly describe a local effect as only Partially supported. The ladder and stakes disagree.

Replacement wording:

> - **Supported** only if published evidence connects infrastructure-attributable substitution and reduced delay in an evaluated population or network whose stated inference covers Edmonton citywide.
> - **Partially supported** if both links are connected only for a corridor or local population, or if citywide evidence establishes only one link.
> - **Contradicted** only if comparable citywide causal or network evidence points against the claimed reduction.
> - **Not established** if neither citywide link is established and no comparable citywide contrary evidence exists.
>
> Local evidence is reported as local; it cannot carry either citywide endpoint verdict.

Previous Claim 2 causal-chain finding: **WEAKENED**. The revision now requires attributable substitution and linked delay evidence, but retains the local-to-citywide leap.

[defect] Claim 1’s coverage denominator is assigned two incompatible meanings, allowing non-qualifying bike corridors to decide its verdict.

Quoted brief text:

Claim 3 instead defines T as:

> “the number of on-street bike-lane corridors in the City’s inventory”

and C as the corridors that actually lost through lanes. Under the shared-calculation instruction, Claim 1 may therefore divide M by all on-street bike corridors, including corridors that do not qualify for Claim 1.

Exact replacement wording:

> In Claim 1, let Q be the number of qualifying converted corridors. Q equals C as established under Claim 3. Let M be the number of those Q corridors with a qualifying published measurement. Replace every Claim 1 reference to T and M/T with Q and M/Q. Claim 3 retains T for the complete on-street inventory and C for qualifying conversions.

[defect] The primary coverage result is unclassified when the one-third alternative is met but the one-half primary threshold is not.

Quoted brief text:

> “Supported requires measurements for at least half of T. The result is reported again using one-third of T as the alternative coverage threshold. If neither coverage threshold is met, the general proposition is Not established.”

If M/Q is 40 per cent, “neither” is false, but the primary one-half threshold is not met. The brief does not assign the primary verdict even though the schema requires one.

Exact replacement wording:

> Apply each coverage threshold independently. For the primary verdict, if M is less than one-half of Q, the result is **Not established**; otherwise apply the verdict ladder below. Separately, for the one-third alternative, if M is less than one-third of Q, the alternative result is **Not established**; otherwise apply the same ladder. Report the one-third result only in `interpretation_notes` or `limitations`.

[framing] “Corridor” is not a defined counting unit.

Quoted brief text:

> “Let T be the number of on-street bike-lane corridors in the City’s inventory”

A corridor is one route identified as a single project with stated endpoints in the City’s project record. Where no project route exists, group contiguous inventory features with the same street name and facility type between documented endpoints; split at a physical gap or change of street. Report the result again using individual inventory features as the reasonable alternative unit and state whether it changes any classification.

## 8. Stakes

Previous stakes finding: **WEAKENED**.

[framing] Claim 2’s stakes say a local effect is only partial, while its current ladder permits Supported; Claim 4’s stakes omit the combined-projects/savings component; and Claim 1’s stakes concern a measured association rather than the asserted cause.

Quoted brief text:

> “Partially supported means only one link or a local effect is established.”

and:

> “Supported means the holder establishes that the documented reason was traffic calming…”

Replacement wording:

> **Claim 1:** Supported means holders gain adequately covered Edmonton evidence attributing the threshold increase in congestion to the lane conversions; opponents must concede that attributable result. Contradicted means comparable adequately covered evidence points against an attributable increase at the fixed cutoff. A mere temporal increase or decrease changes neither side’s causal position.
>
> **Claim 2:** Supported requires the connected causal chain at Edmonton citywide scale. A connected local effect is Partially supported and cannot require either side to concede the citywide generalization.
>
> **Claim 4:** Supported means the holder establishes both that traffic calming would independently have removed the lanes on the required share and that the bike lanes were added to proceeding work to combine projects or save money. Partially supported means only one component or a smaller share is established. Contradicted means comparable records affirmatively point against both components. Not established means the records cannot decide the asserted counterfactual and combination account.

Until those changes are made, a Claim 4 verdict can never resolve the holder’s savings assertion, and a Claim 2 Supported verdict need not surprise an opponent of the citywide claim.
