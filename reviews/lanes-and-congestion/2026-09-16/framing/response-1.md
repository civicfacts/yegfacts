## 2. Claim 4 changes the asserted reason: ADOPTED

The holder said traffic calming, and the brief had let any non-bike reason

## 3. Claim 3 drains "all throughout the city": ADOPTED

Three corridors in two of fifteen districts was too little to hold "all
throughout the city" to. The proposition and thresholds are the checker's:
Supported requires at least one qualifying corridor in at least 8 of the
15 district-plan areas, with 5 as the alternative reported under both;
smaller spreads support the examples or a partial finding, not the
generalisation.

## 3. Claim 1 lets three measured corridors carry a general verdict: ADOPTED

The coverage rule is added as written: T is the total of qualifying
converted corridors under claim 3; Supported also requires measurements
for at least half of T, with one-third of T as the alternative coverage
threshold reported under both; if neither is met the general proposition
is Not established and the measured-corridor results are qualifications.

## 4. Congestion dimensions: ADOPTED

## 4. Claim 2's evidence does not establish its causal chain: ADOPTED

supported; unlinked counts are qualifications; Contradicted requires
comparable causal or network evidence pointing against the reduction;
absence of both is Not established. The household travel survey is named
as context only, as the checker read it.
