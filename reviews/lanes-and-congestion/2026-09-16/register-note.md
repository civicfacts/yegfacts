# Note for the register: one claim carried to another question, one declined at triage

Question: `lanes-and-congestion`. Recorded 2026-09-16 by Stew, after
framing check 1.

## Carried: `79-street-relieves-75-street` (register claim 23)

- **Registered proposition:** "79 Street carries traffic that would
  otherwise use 75 Street and is a school-run route for parents."
- **Captured wording:** [393] Hardy Vole C.: "It is an important street
  for alleviating traffic from 75 St and for people to navigate & drive
  their kids to school before work."
- **Outcome:** removed from this brief on framing check 1, findings 2 and
  7. It is not declined and it carries no finding here.
- **Reason, for publication:** The claim is about what one street does,
  not about what completed lane conversions did to traffic, and the
  register already holds a question about the planned route on 79 Street
  outside the Holyrood school. Road classification, a traffic volume and
  a school's address, which is what this brief had proposed to test it
  with, do not test whether the street takes traffic that would otherwise
  use 75 Street or whether parents drive children to school along it. The
  claim goes to the 79 Street question, to be tested there or dropped
  there for want of a record.
- **What that brief must find, or drop the claim for want of:** for the
  diversion half, an identified published traffic-assignment,
  origin-destination, corridor or diversion study capable of comparing 79
  Street with 75 Street; for the school-trip half, an identified school
  travel plan, traffic assessment or comparable published record of
  school-trip routes. Classification, AAWDT and school proximity are
  qualifications there, not substitutes. Framing check 1 also corrected a
  figure this brief had used: the City's current Complete Streets
  standard puts typical two-lane collectors at 5,000 to 10,000 vehicles
  per day and associates 3,000 with enhanced locals and bikeway
  selection, so 3,000 is not a collector threshold.

## Declined at triage: `roads-carry-goods-and-services` (register claim 22)

Both triage readers declined it independently as a truism accepted by
both sides. The register already carries that outcome and reason on the
claim; nothing changes here. It is listed so the story can say that six
claims were registered under this question and four were sent to the
panel.

## Why this is a note and not an edit

A claim belongs to exactly one question in the register model, and the
register is generated by `scripts/intake-register.ts` and checked by
`scripts/validate.ts`. Moving claim 23 to the 79 Street question is a
register edit that belongs with that question's brief, when it is
drafted, so that the move and the test land together. Until then this
note is the record, committed beside the brief that made the decision,
and the register entry for `lanes-and-congestion` will carry a pointer to
it when this question's lifecycle advances to `briefed`.
