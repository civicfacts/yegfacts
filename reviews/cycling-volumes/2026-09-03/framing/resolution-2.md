# Editor's resolution after framing check 2 (Stew, 2026-09-03)

Under methodology v1.12 the framing check is capped at three reports.
After the second, the editor resolves in writing every finding still OPEN
or WEAKENED, stating what the checker objected to, and the brief is
revised once more; the third report is a confirmation. This is that
resolution.

Check 2 marked eight of twelve findings RESOLVED, four WEAKENED or OPEN,
and raised three new ones. Every one of them is dealt with below. Nine
are adopted, whole or in substance. One is answered by an editorial
decision that leaves the checker's objection standing, and it is set out
here in full so a reader can see the objection as well as the answer.

## Adopted, and now in the brief

**New finding 3, claim 1 had no upper bound.** Adopted. As drafted, any
total above 1.50 million was Partially supported however large it got,
while only a total below 0.65 million could be Contradicted. That is not
symmetric and it is not right: a total three times the stated figure
points against "nearly 1.3 million" as surely as a total a third of it
does. Contradicted now applies below 0.65 million or above 1.95 million,
half again away from the stated figure in either direction, and the
required alternative carries the same outer bounds.

**Earlier 4c, WEAKENED, claim 1's short window.** Adopted in the form the
objection actually demanded. The checker is right that a five-month
subtotal does not test a seven-month proposition, and my first answer let
one do so. The brief now sets a fourteen-day allowance, justified as an
open-data refresh lag rather than a gap in the record, and it splits by
direction: because a short window can only lower a total, Supported,
Partially-supported-above and Contradicted-above all stand on a window
short by up to a fortnight, while Contradicted-below and
Partially-supported-below may not be returned on a short window and give
Not established instead. Short by more than a fortnight is Not
established outright.

**New finding 4, claim 2 had no coverage rule.** Adopted. The draft
quantified over every counter in the verdict set but only defined Not
established when no counter had data at all, which left the common case
undefined. The verdict set is now fixed as the on-street counters in
service on 2025-07-01; a counter is classifiable if it published at least
20 of July's 31 days; all three of Supported, Partially supported and
Contradicted require at least four fifths of the set to be classifiable;
anything less is Not established with every counter's figures reported.

**New finding 4, claim 8 had a hole.** Adopted. The ladder had no
classification where recreation was above 35 percent, commuting was not
larger, and some third purpose category was. The rules are now
exhaustive: Not established where no complete purpose mapping is
possible, and otherwise Supported, Partially supported or Contradicted,
with Contradicted explicitly covering the case the draft left out. The
required alternative has the same structure.

**Earlier 4a, WEAKENED, the CCHS geography was misdescribed.** Adopted,
and the checker was right on a matter of fact. The Canadian Community
Health Survey's public use file publishes health-region geography, not
the City of Edmonton or the Edmonton census metropolitan area. The brief
now says exactly that and makes the survey admissible only if an
identified released product publishes the measure for this brief's
primary geography, otherwise reporting it as an inadmissible geographic
alternative.

**Earlier 2 and 7, WEAKENED, comparing a riding share with lane-use
forms.** Adopted whole, and the checker was right that disclosure does
not make two numerators comparable. My first revision stopped dropping
the two lane-use wordings but then told reviewers to set the
participation share against them, which is the same error wearing a
label. The brief now splits claim 3's eight wordings: the six about
residents who ride carry the verdict; "bike lanes for the .001% of the
population" and "500 people using the bike lanes 4 months of the year"
are reported as made and described as unmeasured, with no participation
figure set against them in either direction. The denominators section
carries the rule as a general one, so it binds everywhere in the brief.

## The finding that stands unresolved, and the decision on it

**Earlier 4b and check-2 finding 6: claims 3 and 4 name no verified
instrument, so the checker would not send them to the panel at all.**

The objection, in the checker's own terms: the source families the brief
names are "hypothetical source classes, not verified instruments"; the
CCHS public-use geography does not match the City or the metropolitan
area; the 2015 household travel survey is a 24-hour diary rather than a
participation survey; and claim 4 needs one instrument publishing both a
year-round and a non-winter share from the same Edmonton sample, which
nothing identified does. His remedy is to send each claim to the panel
only if such an instrument is named, and otherwise to drop it as
uncheckable while keeping its wording and reason in the record.

I have done the work he asked for rather than deferring it. Drafting and
revising this brief I searched for a representative Edmonton
twelve-month cycling-participation measure and did not identify one. The
CCHS public use file is out on geography, as he says. The 2015 household
travel survey is a 24-hour diary, as he says. The Navigating Tomorrow
successor survey launched in September 2025, runs continuously, and had
published no results by the as-of date. What remains unruled is the
City's own bike ridership survey series, one of which was analysed in the
peer-reviewed literature and one of which has produced a published
seasonal cycling share; I could not establish its sampling method from
the published material available to me. That search is now written into
the brief, so reviewers start from it and can correct it, and the brief
says plainly that a reviewer who finds an instrument I missed makes that
instrument carry the verdict and this note wrong.

**The decision: both claims go to the panel, with Not established
predeclared as a real outcome.** Three reasons.

First, the check's own bound is aimed at a different thing. It says a
definition predetermines Not established, and is itself a defect, when
the brief proposes a threshold, unit or calculation no published source
could supply. The proposition here is not the brief's invention. Eight
people asserted a share of the population, in those words, and the brief
is required by the standing rule above check 1 to test the claim as its
holders assert it. If no instrument publishes it, that is a fact about
the record, not a choice the framing made.

Second, Not established here has content. The reviewer prompt says in
terms that "the public record cannot answer this" is a valuable finding
rather than a failure, and requires the absence to be bounded: what was
searched, for what place and period, as of when. The brief requires
exactly that. A reader who has seen a population share quoted eight times
in one thread learns something real from being told that nobody publishes
it and being shown where it was looked for. The site also has machinery
for the case where that is all a question yields: methodology v1.13 moves
a story whose claims are all Not established off the findings board and
onto the claims considered, with its reason. That machinery runs after a
panel, on evidence, not before one on a prediction.

Third, and decisive against dropping: I have not established that the
instrument does not exist. I have established that I did not find it.
Dropping the second-most-argued claim in this question on that basis
would be the editor deciding, on an incomplete search, that a question
twenty-five people are arguing about gets no answer. That is the
selection this method was rebuilt in v1.15 to stop doing. The one claim
this brief does drop, `heritage-days-bike-arrivals`, is dropped because
no record of that kind could exist, which is a different thing from a
record I could not find.

The cost of being wrong here is one panel run returning Not established
on two claims, with a bounded absence published. The cost of being wrong
the other way is silence on the figure this argument is mostly conducted
in. I take the first.

The checker's objection stands on the record in `check-2.md`. Ildar
Abdulin remains accountable for the result and can revert it. If the
panel returns Not established on both claims and a later source turns up,
the claims are rerun rather than corrected in place.

## What check 3 is being asked

Check 3 is a confirmation. Its job is to say whether the revisions above
meet the findings they answer, and to record this resolution as the
editor's disposition of the one that stands. FRAME OK freezes the brief
with the resolution beside it. REVISE parks the brief, and it reopens
only on new intake evidence.
