## How this question was formed

The claims were extracted by three model seats (`flash`, `haiku`, `luna`)
reading the whole thread, merged, grouped into questions, and triaged by
two seats that had not seen each other's answers. The run is at
`reviews/intake/yegscoop-2026-08-26`.

The question triage returned GO from both seats:

> Municipal budgets and intergovernmental funding records can show how much
> road money comes from property taxes, fuel-tax transfers and other
> sources shared by drivers and cyclists.

Fifteen distinct accounts took part, seven on the side that says everybody
pays and eight on the side that says drivers pay. That is the most evenly
split question in the source, and it is why it was registered.

## Which claims the brief carries, and why the other two do not

The brief carries claims 1 and 2. It carries no verdict on claims 3 and 4.

Both intake triage seats independently returned NO on both, and their
reasons are the same reason:

> `cyclists-are-taxpayers` — NO — "Residents paying general taxes while
> also riding bicycles is an ordinary feature of the tax system that
> neither side meaningfully disputes."

> `we-pay-for-services-we-dont-use` — NO — "Municipal taxes funding shared
> services regardless of each resident's personal use is an undisputed
> feature of public finance."

Both are true, neither is disputed by anyone in the thread, and a panel
run on either would produce a finding that tells no reader anything. That
is the failure the site named in methodology v1.19 and took four published
claims off the findings board for. Putting them in this brief would repeat
it deliberately.

The register's data model gives a claim no state of its own, so there is
at present no field in which to record that a question's brief does not
carry one of its claims. That gap is written up in `run-record.md` as an
open question for the register, and this file is the record in the
meantime. Nothing was deleted: both claims keep their register entries,
their wordings and their accounts.

## What is not in this question

The thread contains adjacent claims that the intake grouped under other
questions, and they are not intake for this one:

- `bike-share-one-percent-of-road-budget` (question `active-transportation`):
  what share of the road capital budget the bike-lane money is.
- `basic-services-in-poor-condition` (question `infrastructure-deficit`):
  whether Edmonton's roads and alleys are in poor condition.
- `free-parking-is-a-subsidy` (question `on-street-parking`): whether free
  on-street parking is a subsidy.
- `most-cyclists-also-drive` (question `who-cycles`): whether most people
  who cycle also drive.

The two bicycle-licensing proposals in comments 373 and 439, and the
fairness argument about who deserves to pay, are preferences and value
judgements. The site does not test those.
