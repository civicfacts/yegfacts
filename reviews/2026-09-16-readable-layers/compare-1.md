## Across all seven

**Overall:** New wins **4–3**, but narrowly. It is much better at first-minute reading: shorter top layers, one-fact bullets, and better bridges into the explanation. But its three losses are not cosmetic: Cycling Volumes muddies a verification caveat, Electric Buses states more than the evidence supports, and Infill hides a material panel disagreement. Those are core fact-checking regressions. I would not ship New unchanged.

The three biggest remaining obstacles are: **first**, every page opens with "YEGFacts has not launched yet… We have not yet found the right methodology." That tells a skeptical resident to distrust the product before seeing its work. **Second**, the answer is immediately followed by the provenance slab — "Verdicts by a three-model AI panel" and "so no person chooses the finding" — plus dates and methodology metadata before the TL;DR. Useful information, wrong position. **Third**, Cycling Volumes makes the reader traverse seven claim cards before reaching its page-level synthesis.

The three best things not to undo: putting primary-source language high — "the choice will remain to make those trips by auto"; making TL;DR bullets say one human-sized fact — "Nobody has measured what share of Edmonton's own trips are made by bike in winter"; and adding narrative bridges such as "Edmonton bought 60 electric buses… This page follows the buses and the money."

**One structural change:** make the top sequence **title → claim(s) → answer → TL;DR**, uninterrupted. Move provenance, status/process notes and methodology metadata into a compact "How this was checked" block immediately after the TL;DR. That preserves transparency without putting the machinery between the resident and the evidence.

**Editor: all three obstacles and the structural change adopted.** The banner now says the method is being worked out in the open and every change is logged, which is true, instead of that the site has not found the right methodology, which told a reader to leave. The provenance line and the board notices sit after the TL;DR, in the same words. On pages with several claims the strip follows the TL;DR. The three things not to undo are untouched, except that "Nobody has measured" is "Nobody has published", the bound the faithfulness check of 2026-09-04 set.
