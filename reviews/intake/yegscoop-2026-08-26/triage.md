# Triage, two seats

Three propositions that name an identifiable person were declined and appear here
under a neutral id, without their wording. The site does not repeat an
allegation it has not checked and has no way to put to the person.

99 propositions. Seats: sol, gemini. Both read the same list; neither saw the other.

GO takes both readers, or one GO and one PARK. NO takes both. Everything else
parks, including a straight GO against NO: a claim one reader would refuse does
not jump the queue, and one reader's refusal does not discard it either.

- agreed on 58 of 99
- GO 61, PARK 30, NO 8
