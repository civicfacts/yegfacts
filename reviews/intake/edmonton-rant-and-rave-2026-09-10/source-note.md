# Source note, given to every seat in this run

The committed intake prompts define `side` as a position in "the bike-lane
spending argument", because the first whole-source intake was a bike-lane
thread. This source is not. Rather than edit the prompts, which are outside
this run's footprint, the paragraph below was appended to each assembled
prompt, verbatim, directly after the prompt text and before the material the
seat reads. It describes what the people in this source are arguing about; it
does not name any claim, choose any, or say which are true.

> **Source note for this run.** This source is not about bike lanes. It is a
> post in a public Edmonton Facebook group complaining that the City's garbage
> collection and snow clearing are not worth what residents pay, and the
> argument in the comments is about whether Edmonton's waste service (one black
> bin every two weeks, bin sizes, the fees for it), its snow clearing and its
> spending are adequate for what residents pay in taxes and utility fees. Read
> every reference to "the bike-lane spending argument" in the instructions as
> a reference to that argument. `against` means the claim serves the position
> that the City's service is inadequate, overpriced, or that the money is
> misspent; `for` means the claim serves the position that the service is
> adequate, the fees are fair or separate from taxes, or the complaint rests on
> a mistake; `neither` means neither. Bike lanes, infill and other City
> spending come up as comparisons, and a claim about them is still a claim
> about Edmonton civic government; give it the side it serves in this
> argument.

Recorded here because a prompt that hard-codes one source's argument is a
defect the second source exposed. The fix belongs in `prompts/intake-*.md` and
a methodology entry, not in this run.
