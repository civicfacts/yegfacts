# Source note, given to every seat in this run

Under methodology v1.33 each whole-source run commits one paragraph naming
the argument the source is having and what `for` and `against` mean in it.
The intake prompts point at it and name no source themselves. This is that
paragraph for this run, appended verbatim to every assembled prompt after
the prompt text and before the material the seat reads. It describes the
source; it names no claim, chooses none, and says nothing about what is true.

> **Source note for this run.** This source is a post in a public Edmonton
> Facebook group complaining that the City's garbage collection and snow
> clearing are not worth what residents pay. The argument in the comments is
> about whether Edmonton's waste service (one black bin every two weeks, bin
> sizes, the fees for it), its snow clearing and its spending are adequate
> for what residents pay in taxes and utility fees. `against` means the claim
> serves the position that the City's service is inadequate, overpriced, or
> that the money is misspent; `for` means the claim serves the position that
> the service is adequate, the fees are fair or separate from taxes, or the
> complaint rests on a mistake; `neither` means neither. Bike lanes, infill
> and other City spending come up as comparisons, and a claim about them is
> still a claim about Edmonton civic government; give it the side it serves
> in this argument.

The two extraction seats that ran on 2026-09-16 before the prompts were
changed received the same paragraph with one extra sentence telling them to
read the prompt's "bike-lane spending argument" as this one. Those
extractions were discarded and every seat was re-run under the changed
prompts, so nothing in this run rests on the earlier wording.
