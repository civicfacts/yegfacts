## Magnitude re-run

Recorded 2026-09-02 by Stew.

This directory is a further re-run of Claim 1, `ip-teardown-price-gap`,
alone. It is not triggered by a panel framing concern. It is triggered by
the published proposition itself.

The proposition the panel was given asked whether the housing built on a
teardown lot is worth more than the house that came down. No informed
person disputes that. New housing on an Edmonton lot is newer, usually
larger and usually denser than the dwelling demolished for it, and a
verdict on that proposition, whichever way it fell, tells a reader almost
nothing about the thing being argued over. Five framing-check rounds
across three runs narrowed the brief toward a form the record could answer,
and the magnitude the captured quotation actually asserts, a modest house
replaced by housing priced near a million, was drained out of it a step at
a time. The editor adopted every one of those steps.

The founder read the published page and said of the proposition it tested:

> "that's poor claim to make"

The editor agrees. The captured quotation is not a direction claim, it is a
magnitude claim: "A $350,000 house is torn down and a $1,000,000 house goes
up in its place." Roughly three times, on the figures as given. That is the
proposition this brief tests, with a predeclared threshold and one
alternative, under methodology v1.10, whose check 3 now rejects a
proposition no informed person disputes as a framing defect in its own
right.

Claim 2, `ip-infill-affordable`, is not re-run here. Its proposition
already carries a magnitude, the 30 percent shelter-cost-to-income
threshold, and nothing in the founder's objection or in the v1.10 change
touches it. Its published finding stands.

The published finding on `ip-teardown-price-gap` also stands until this
re-run replaces it. This brief carries the same claim id, so the re-run's
result replaces the published finding as a verdict change or an updated
entry rather than creating a second claim.

The raw claim, the provenance and the limits on it are unchanged from the
intake above. No new circulating form was captured for this re-run.
