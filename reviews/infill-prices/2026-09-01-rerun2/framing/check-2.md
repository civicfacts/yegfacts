### The tenure rule does not keep Claim 2 checkable

The brief acknowledges:

> No published source supplies unit-level tenure, contract rent and utility inclusion for the declared cohort

It then requires:

> Where a dwelling unit's tenure cannot be established from a published source, that dwelling unit is unclassified under the bounds rule below

This removes the title-based error, but moves the unavailable input from contract rent to tenure. Edmonton's published ownership data are aggregated to neighbourhood level and explicitly withhold individual-residence information. Alberta title records establish ownership and title structure, not whether a dwelling is rented, owner occupied, or unlet. I therefore find no plausible published source that can classify tenure systematically across the declared property-level cohort. This is an inference from the fields those sources publish. [Edmonton dwelling ownership data](https://data.edmonton.ca/Census/2012-Census-Dwelling-Unit-By-Ownership-Neighbourho/ht22-msjv), [Alberta land-title contents](https://www.alberta.ca/find-land-titles-documents-plans.aspx)

### The claimed CMHC utility field does not exist

The brief says:

> take utility inclusion from the same CMHC Rental Market Survey cell that supplied the modelled rent, which states for that cell whether the rent includes utilities

CMHC publishes Edmonton average-rent cells by bedroom count and geography, but its methodology says those rents are not adjusted for utility inclusion and that heat, electricity, and hot water may or may not be included. The published cell does not provide the inclusion statement the brief requires. Under the brief's next sentence, this makes every rented unit using that model unclassified. [CMHC rent cells](https://www03.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/hmip-pimh/en/TableMapChart/TableMatchingCriteria?CategoryLevel1=Primary+Rental+Market&CategoryLevel2=Average+Rent+%28%24%29&ColumnField=2&GeographyId=0340&GeographyType=MetropolitanMajorArea&RowField=24), [CMHC Rental Market Survey methodology](https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/housing-markets-data-and-research/housing-research/surveys/methods/methodology-rental-market-survey)
