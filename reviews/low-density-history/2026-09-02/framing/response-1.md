# Response to framing check 1

Brief: reviews/low-density-history/2026-09-02/brief.md, Revision 1 after
framing check 1. Every numbered finding in framing/check-1.md is answered
below in the checker's order.

## 1. Provenance: three editorial paraphrases presented as circulating forms; unsupported prevalence claims

Changed. The brief now carries the checker's replacement wording verbatim
under a new "Circulating forms" section:

> **Founder-supplied form:** "Proper management and fewer pet projects is a
> good start. We've had low density housing in this city for 100 years.
> Many neighbourhoods were planned around it." The platform, author and URL
> were not captured. The wording may be a composite paraphrase rather than
> a verbatim post. No other circulating forms were captured.
>
> **Prevalence:** Unknown from this intake. It contains one founder-supplied
> formulation.

The three paraphrases are retained but moved to a separate block and
labelled:

> **Site-written formulations, not captured posts.** The three sentences
> below were written by the site as editorial interpretations of how the
> premise might be put. They are not evidence of how the claim circulates,
> they were not observed anywhere, and reviewers must not treat them as
> source material or as part of either proposition:

The selection rationale's prevalence bullet was rewritten:

> - Prevalence: unknown from this intake. The intake contains one
>   founder-supplied formulation, described by the founder as a reply in an
>   Edmonton infill or zoning thread. Because no source sample was
>   captured, this brief cannot establish how prevalent or representative
>   that wording is, and reviewers should not treat prevalence as
>   established.

"Keep returning" and "one of the most repeated moves" are gone from the
story and the rationale.

## 2. Four assertions folded into one proposition

Changed. The single proposition is replaced by the checker's two, with the
two claim ids the brief now carries:

> ### Claim A, id: ldh-existed-century
> **Normalized proposition:** Low-density housing has existed in Edmonton
> since approximately 1926.
>
> ### Claim B, id: ldh-planned-around
> **Normalized proposition:** Many Edmonton neighbourhoods were planned
> around low-density housing during that period.
>
> Review and report the two propositions separately, each with its own
> verdict, confidence and evidence-basis label. Do not treat "low density"
> as synonymous with "detached", or "many" as synonymous with "most". A
> finding about Claim A is not by itself a finding about Claim B.

Assertions 3 and 4 are dropped. The story section's claim list now names
`ldh-existed-century` and `ldh-planned-around` only.

## 3. Not the strongest fair reading ("detached", "most")

Changed. "Detached" is no longer used as a synonym for low density anywhere
in the brief, and "most" is gone. The checker's clarifier is in the
operationalization:

> "Planned around" does not require exclusive detached-house zoning unless
> the source evidence establishes that narrower proposition.

## 4a. Meaning of "planned around" limited to public instruments

Changed to the checker's wording:

> Evaluate "planned around" using both public planning instruments and
> other documented evidence of deliberate neighbourhood planning, including
> subdivision design, developer plans, restrictive covenants, servicing
> standards and street or lot layouts. Report these evidence classes
> separately. Built form alone does not establish planning intent, but it
> may show whether a documented plan was implemented.

## 4b. Accounting window

Changed. Both windows are required, and every finding is reported under
both:

> Use two reported windows, and report every finding under both:
> 1. The exact 100-year period ending on the freeze date recorded in
>    run.yaml.
> 2. The period beginning with Edmonton's first verified zoning bylaw.
>
> Include earlier non-zoning planning instruments where available. Do not
> describe the regulatory lineage as continuous across years when no
> applicable instrument has been established.

## 4c. Definition of low density

Changed. Both definitions are required:

> Report both of the following, and report each finding under both:
> 1. Each era's own terminology, quoted from the instrument in force ...
> 2. Consistent cross-era measures, including permitted housing types,
>    permitted units per lot, any available unit-density measure (units per
>    hectare or per acre), minimum lot area and frontage, and site
>    coverage.
>
> Do not equate "low density" with detached-only housing. If no neutral
> threshold can be justified before reviewing the results, report findings
> under the reasonable alternative definitions and say which definition
> produces which result.

## 4d. The departure comparison

Changed. The entire "A departure" paragraph is deleted. The brief now says:

> Any claim that the zoning in force in 2026 is a historical departure
> requires its own captured source and its own brief, with its own cutoff
> date and comparison rules.

and lists "whether the zoning in force in 2026 departs from historical
rules" under Out of scope.

## 4e. Denominator and "many"

Changed to the checker's wording:

> For each era, report both the number and share of then-existing
> City-defined neighbourhoods covered, plus the share of residential land
> where the necessary boundary data exists. State the neighbourhood and
> land denominators, the boundary year and the treatment of partial
> coverage. Report conclusions under both denominators. Do not convert
> "many" into "most", and do not select one denominator after seeing the
> results.

## 4f. As-of date and version

Changed, with the freeze date named as the checker required:

> Where a finding refers to the zoning bylaw currently in force, identify
> the bylaw and the official consolidation in force on the freeze date
> recorded in run.yaml. State that date and the version reviewed.

## 4g. "Required calculations: none"

Changed to the checker's wording:

> - Neighbourhood counts and shares, residential-land coverage shares where
>   data permits, and any housing-mix figures used. For each calculation,
>   state the numerator, denominator, geography, boundary year, source and

The instrument-citation and housing-mix requirements from the earlier draft
are kept alongside it.

## 4h. External comparator (checker's note, no change required)

The checker said no external comparator is required and that one would not
determine whether Edmonton's own record establishes the propositions. The
brief now says exactly that:

> No external comparator is required. Both propositions are about
> Edmonton's own record, and another jurisdiction's record cannot establish
> them.

The transferability requirement is kept for the one case the checker
allowed, a model bylaw Edmonton adopted from elsewhere used to interpret an
Edmonton instrument.

## 5. Leaked expected finding

Changed. Both "evidence that would support" and "evidence that would
contradict" paragraphs are deleted and replaced with the checker's neutral

> Examine the relevant instruments, maps, subdivision records and outcome
> data across the full period. For each source, report what it establishes
> about planning intent, permitted housing forms, geographic coverage and
> built outcomes. Apply the same search effort and evidentiary standards
> across eras and residential districts.

The old reviewer instruction "Search for instruments that cut against the
proposition with the same diligence as for those that fit it" is also gone,
since it framed the search around a proposition the reviewers were expected
to be testing against.

Changed to the checker's wording:
