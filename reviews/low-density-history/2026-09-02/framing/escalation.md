## Open findings and proposed resolutions (Stew, 2026-09-02)

1. **Exact-day test of an approximate claim.** Adopt the checker's
   wording: report at the exact-date cutoff and for calendar year 1926;
   separate variant verdicts if they differ; an exact-day failure alone
   does not decide "approximately 1926".
2. **Units-per-lot is not the City's density measure.** Adopt: Claim A
   classifies documented built outcome in dwellings per hectare with the
   observed housing forms reported; Claim B classifies permitted or
   intended development using each era's own term plus one predeclared
   cross-era density measure, with the threshold's source identified;
   all permitted residential forms counted, not only the principal use.
3. **"Many" collapsed into a conjunctive rule.** Adopt: count and share
   are separate variants with separate verdicts; no single unqualified
   verdict when they differ; for each cutoff, either an identified
   pre-existing standard or one stated alternative with results under
   both.
4. **Neighbourhood unit.** Adopt: present-day polygons plus, where
   boundaries are recoverable, contemporaneous subdivisions; predeclared
   handling of splits and overlaps; limitation reported where not
   recoverable; separate verdicts if the unit changes the result.
5. **No aggregation rule for Claim B over the window.** Adopt: city-wide
   bylaw periods are the eras; neighbourhood plans assigned to them by
   operative date; Claim B tested at the end of the window and
   cumulatively across it, both reported.
6. **Coverage threshold and occupied-neighbourhood denominator.** Adopt:
   exact covered share reported; results under the 50 percent rule and
   an area-weighted calculation; planned units reported separately from
   occupied neighbourhoods at era end.
7. **Pre-annexation neighbourhoods.** Adopt the second option: limit
   Claim B's proposition and geography to Edmonton's historical
   boundaries and remove pre-annexation planning from the calculation,
   reporting it as context only. Simpler, and the post is about "this
   city".

## Stew's note on the check itself

Every finding above is a real verdict-sensitive choice. But finding 3
also shows a regress: a predeclared cutoff is asked for an alternative,
and the alternative would then need one too. I propose a one-line bound
in the prompt: a cutoff justified from an identified standard, or
stated with one alternative and results under both, is sufficient. That
is a prompt change (v1.8) and is listed for your decision separately.
