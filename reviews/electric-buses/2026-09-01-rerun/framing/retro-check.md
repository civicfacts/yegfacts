## 4. Operationalization and its alternatives

**Finding.**

The brief leaves verdict-sensitive choices unresolved.

For Claim 1:

- "Substantially" and "material" have no thresholds.
- Contract specifications, public expectations, and actual service requirements are different comparators.
- Range could mean fleet mean, median, minimum, route-adjusted range, or performance under the contract's test conditions.
- Reliability could use fleet availability, vehicle kilometres between failures, missed pull-outs, or completed service hours. Each has a different denominator.
- Durability could mean defect incidence, repair cost, downtime, or safety effect.
- Vendor support could mean compliance with contractual obligations or practical access to parts and repairs.
- The brief does not say whether one failed dimension establishes the proposition or whether all four must fail.

Separate these propositions. Predefine each measure and denominator. If contract and operational benchmarks can produce different findings, require both results.

For Claim 2:

- Gross public expenditure and net economic loss are different measures.
- "Public" could mean Edmonton alone or all governments combined.
- Charging infrastructure could be fully attributed, allocated across its useful life, or valued by remaining shared use.
- Service delivered could be valued through market value, avoided replacement service, depreciation, or another method.
- Residual value could use book value, market value, salvage value, or remaining service potential.
- "Non-routine repairs" has no boundary.
- "Approximately $82 million" has no tolerance.
- Currency conversion lacks an exchange-rate source and date.
- A 2026-08-31 cutoff excludes later recoveries while the proposition states an unqualified net loss. Final bankruptcy recovery or fleet retirement could change the result.

Require separate gross-outlay and net-loss calculations. Report municipal and consolidated-government totals. Predefine valuation, allocation, currency, and approximation rules. Either report the 2026-08-31 result explicitly as an interim snapshot or include later recoveries and remaining asset value.

Replace:

With:

For Claim 3:

- "Suitable" needs winter range, availability, route completion, or another service threshold.
- "In general" needs a denominator and decision rule.
- The comparator class could include every battery-electric bus, only the relevant technology generation, or systems with similar routes and charging arrangements.
- A single counterexample defeats "cannot work anywhere" but does not necessarily defeat "generally unsuitable." The brief currently mixes those readings.
- Results may change by manufacturer, vehicle generation, heating system, charging method, duty cycle, and period.

Pre-register the comparator class, metrics, denominator, transferability rules, and decision rule. Report materially different results under reasonable alternative comparator classes.
