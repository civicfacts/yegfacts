### Check 4(b). The green-cart reopening instrument is overstated [framing]

**What the checker said.** Park 5 said "the trucks' cart-tip records
would hold" a seasonal set-out rate. The City's 2024 Waste Collections
Audit describes tip records and collector-entered "not out" events but
finds the noncollection data incomplete and inaccurate, and a tip count
alone does not tell a cart left at home from one the crew missed.

**Resolution: accepted; replacement applied verbatim.** I read the audit
from the City's address and ingested it as YF-EV-0347 (28 pages, SHA-256
`0787c5ea…1f3b`; the cover dates the report August 15, 2024). It confirms the description: carts carry radio tags
that record each tip; collectors press one of three buttons, spacing,
"Not out" or non-compliant, to say why a cart was not tipped; the auditor
found "incomplete and inaccurate non-collection data" and no data
assurance process, and that the City "is not able to use the aggregate
non-collection data". Park 5's parenthesis and reopening condition are
replaced with your text word for word. The same parenthetical lead,
"the cart-tip records the City's trucks collect by radio tag", is
removed from park 1's list of places to look, for the same reason.
