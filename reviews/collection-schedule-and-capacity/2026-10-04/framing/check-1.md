### 7. Scope traps — [framing]

The brief justifies curbside scope because “the commenters describe their own bins at their own homes,” while the post author specifically says **condo** and **small black bins**. Condo status alone does not identify the service class: the City describes some condo units receiving individual curbside carts, and Bylaw 20363 defines curbside service by assigned individual carts. The central household must not silently become an assumed curbside or assumed communal example. See the [City’s apartment and condo service description](https://www.edmonton.ca/programs_services/garbage_waste/apartment-condo-collection) and [bylaw definition](https://www.edmonton.ca/sites/default/files/public-files/assets/Bylaws/BL20363.pdf).

**Replace with:** “The post author reports small bins at a condo; the capture does not establish whether that household receives curbside or communal collection. Schedule findings for curbside homes must not be presented as findings about that household.”

The separate tax, billing, missed-pickup, sorting and policy questions are otherwise identified as separate stories.
