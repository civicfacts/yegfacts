### Finding 1. Park 4, the diaper claim [framing]

**What the checker said.** The current condition lets a broad
age-category share of garbage or a City capacity guideline reopen a claim
whose point is that two children in diapers add enough garbage to
overflow an assigned container. Neither tests that consequence.

**Conceded.** The current condition is weaker than the claim, and I wrote
in the same park that the City's cart-suitability line cannot grade
adequacy, so a guideline should not reopen it either.

**Sentence it replaces, as submitted:** "It reopens if a characterization
study reports diapers' share of garbage-cart contents by volume for
households with young children, or if the City publishes a capacity
guideline for such households." (The report quotes it with a curly
apostrophe; the brief has a straight one.)

**Replacement text, exactly as the checker supplied it:**

> It reopens only if a published household-level study links diaper volume and total residual garbage for households with two children in diapers to their assigned container capacity and collection interval, and reports whether they overflow more often than comparable households without children in diapers. A City capacity guideline may be given as background but does not by itself reopen the factual overflow claim. Any later verdict test must predeclare what counts as ‘a lot more’ before examining the result.

### Finding 2. Park 6, the twenty-bag claim [framing]

**What the checker said.** The brief defines the claim's point as the
combined assertion that renovators used the unlimited service to set out
about twenty bags a week, but the current condition lets either half
reopen it alone.

**Conceded.** The park says the point is the amount and who; a condition
that reopens on one of the two answers half the claim.

**Sentence it replaces, as submitted:** "It reopens if a City record from
before the cart system reports the distribution of bags set out per
home, or renovation waste found in residential collection."

**Replacement text, exactly as the checker supplied it:**

> It reopens only if a City record from the pre-cart period reports how many residential households set out about 20 bags in a week under the unlimited rule and establishes that those set-outs included renovation waste. A bag-count distribution without waste type, or a renovation-waste measure without bag counts, is background for a separate component and does not reopen the combined claim.
