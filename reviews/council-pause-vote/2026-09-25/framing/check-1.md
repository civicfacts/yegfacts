Reviewers report the destinations in the motions’ words. They do not decide whether those destinations are ‘core priorities’.”

- [framing] **“Future” disappears from claim 2’s decision rule.** The proposition requires “14 named, unbuilt routes,” but construction status appears only in a qualification; the Supported rule does not require it. Replace the Supported condition with one that requires the 14 counted routes to meet the brief’s stated *unbuilt* definition, using Attachment 3 and any dated update needed to establish status at the recommendation date. If status cannot be established, do not silently award Supported.

- [framing] **Claim 1 combines accounts of two different bodies.** Two captured comments allege that council ignored “a committee’s decision”; three describe council declining administration’s recommendation. The brief says a mismatch about bodies is merely “a qualification rather than testing a claim nobody could verify.” Whether the committee made a decision, and whether full Council later overrode it, are questions the meeting record can test. Replace that sentence with: “Report separately what Infrastructure Committee decided and what, if anything, City Council later decided. Do not describe a committee vote as a City Council vote or an override without a later, incompatible Council decision.” The single claim verdict still needs one declared primary body.

### 3. Strongest fair reading — findings

- [framing] **Claim 2 can call re-evaluation a freeze without a hold.** The primary rule accepts “re-evaluation, evaluation for trade-offs, or a report for Council direction” as meeting “would not proceed pending a reassessment.” Those activities alone do not say construction must wait. A holder asserted *freezing* routes; an opponent could accept further evaluation while denying a freeze. Replace the primary rule with: “The recommended approach expressly defers construction or advancement of the listed routes until a further evaluation, report, or decision.” Keep a separately reported alternative for removal from programme scope or an express “will not proceed” instruction. Do not require a stated end date if the hold is indefinite.

- [framing] **“Brought forward together” is too narrow.** The quotation “Councillor Karen Principe and I brought forward two motions — twice” does not say both co-sponsored each motion. The brief requires one to move and the other to second each one. Replace the primary reading with: “Count a motion if either Principe or Rice moved or seconded it. Report each person’s role, whether both participated in the same motion, and whether the two attempts occurred on separate occasions.” Report the current co-sponsorship reading as an alternative.

- [framing] **The “same seven” test permits a changing, thinly observed group.** Under “The bloc B,” someone qualifies after two favourable votes, regardless of absences or whether they served before and after the 2025 election. Seven qualifying names need never have voted together. That is weaker than “the same old 7 councillors.” Replace the primary proposition and rule with a fixed set of seven people who each voted on the programme’s side at every eligible programme vote while serving, and require the brief to say whether “same old” means the same seven must have served and voted on both sides of the election. Report a less strict absence rule as an alternative, not as the verdict-carrying reading. The City’s published election results and minutes can establish membership and votes; this does not require an unavailable instrument. [The City’s election-results page](https://www.edmonton.ca/city_government/municipal_elections/election-history-and-results) identifies the two council terms.

### 8. Stakes — finding

The existing Stakes section names both sides, but its consequences sometimes outrun the verdict rules. Rewrite it so each outcome means the following:

- **Pause decision.** Supported would establish that the named body *rejected a hold* and an opponent could no longer say it merely took no decision. Contradicted would establish that the body adopted the hold and the holder would have to withdraw the non-support claim. A committee result must not be called a full Council result.
- **Administration’s recommendation.** Supported would establish an actual hold on 14 unbuilt routes, not only a plan to study them. Contradicted would establish that no such hold was recommended; the holder would lose the asserted treatment, even if some re-evaluation was proposed.
- **Budget motions.** Supported would establish two attempts by the named councillors to leave about $50 million, direct the remainder as stated in the motions, and lose both votes. Contradicted would establish that the recorded attempts did not occur or were not defeated. A result against mandatory co-sponsorship alone would not refute what the holder said.
- **Same seven.** Supported would establish the *same seven people* on the programme’s side across the defined eligible votes; an opponent could no longer describe the pattern as shifting. Contradicted would establish that no such fixed seven exists; the holder would lose the claimed number and pattern. Seven people each favourable in only two votes would not surprise that opponent.

Replace the current stakes sentences that claim more than the rules establish, especially “a bloc of at least seven members has in fact voted ... in every recorded vote.”
