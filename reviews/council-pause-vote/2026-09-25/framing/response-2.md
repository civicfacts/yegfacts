against the programme. A Contradicted that follows from an empty term

is no evidence either way, which is what Not established exists for. So
the brief says that in that case the primary rule reports Not established,
names the empty term, and reports the alternatives as usual. This makes
the primary rule no easier to satisfy; it only stops a want of votes from
being read as a vote against.
