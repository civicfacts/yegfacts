## Item 4: account splits

1. **High — Promise**

   Objected sentence:

   > The validator checks the total two ways: it must fall inside the range its claims allow, and the split must add up to it, so a person who argued on more than one side is counted once, on the side their own words put them on.

   The arithmetic check proves only that the buckets add to the total. It cannot prove that each person appears once. A duplicate and an omission could cancel each other. It also cannot prove that a person who argued on multiple sides was assigned to the right one, because the register stores neither their identity nor the reason for the choice.

   Rewrite:

   > The validator checks the total two ways: it must fall inside the range its claims allow, and the for, against and neither counts must add up to it. Editors must count each person in only one of those groups.

   The first sentence is licensed by the two implemented arithmetic checks. The second states an editorial rule without pretending the validator enforces or explains it.
