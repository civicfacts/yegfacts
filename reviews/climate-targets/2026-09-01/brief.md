# Review brief: Edmonton's climate targets

Status: PROVISIONAL — drafted by the orchestrator 2026-09-01; founder
ratifies at the gate. Frozen before round 1. Methodology: v1.0.

## Story

Edmonton has adopted community greenhouse-gas reduction targets (Energy
Transition Strategy, carbon budget). City communications describe climate
action and progress. This story checks whether Edmonton is on track against
its own adopted targets, using the City's own published measurements.

## Why this story (selection rationale)

- Prevalence: "the City says it's taking climate action" vs "targets are
  being missed" both circulate; residents cannot easily check either.
- Civic importance: targets drive budget decisions (carbon budget process).
- Verifiability: unusually high — the City publishes its own GHG
  inventories and has itself reported on-track/off-track status.
- Editorial balance: expected finding cuts against City messaging, which
  demonstrates the site is not an administration mouthpiece.

## Claim under review

### Claim — id: climate-on-track

**Circulating forms:** "Edmonton is a climate leader." "The City is
meeting its climate goals." Conversely: "the targets are fake, nothing is
on track."

**Normalized proposition:** Edmonton is on track to meet its adopted
community greenhouse-gas emissions targets.

**Operationalization:** "On track" means the most recent published
community emissions inventory is at or below the trajectory required by
the City's adopted targets (2025/2030/2050 milestones as defined in the
Energy Transition Strategy and carbon budget documents), OR the City's own
published assessment states it is on track. Use the City's own inventory
and budget documents as primary evidence. Corporate (City-operations)
emissions are a different measure from community emissions — do not
conflate them; report both if sources allow, but the claim is about the
community targets. If the City's own documents state it is not on track,
that is strong direct evidence. Note the vintage of the latest inventory.

## Scope

Geography: Edmonton (community-wide emissions). Time: adopted targets as
of 2026 and the latest published inventory. Out of scope: whether the
targets are good policy, federal/provincial policy, future projections
beyond what City documents state.

## Required calculations

Latest inventory value vs target-trajectory value for the nearest
milestone year, as reported in City documents — cite both numbers with
document and page/section; no independent trajectory modelling.

## Reviewer instructions of special note

- The strongest evidence here is the City contradicting its own goal;
  quote it precisely and date it.
- Check for anything more recent than the sources you first find — a 2025
  or 2026 inventory or budget statement supersedes older reporting.
- Record every source URL; you have no repo access — cite fully.
