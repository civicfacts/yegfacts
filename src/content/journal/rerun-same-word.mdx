The comment says a $350,000 house is torn down and a $1,000,000 house goes
up in its place. Is that typical? [Three reviewers](/facts/infill-prices#ip-teardown-price-gap)
tried to find out, twice, and came back Not established, unanimously.

Here is why that is the interesting part. Edmonton publishes assessed values
for every property and permits for every demolition, but nothing that joins
the house that came down to what was built on its lot. One reviewer joined
eight lots by hand and found ratios from about 1.4 to about 3.9. Eight lots
is an anecdote, and the reviewer said so.

So the most repeated number in the city's infill argument has no public
check behind it, for either side. If you are quoting it, you are guessing.
If you are dismissing it, so are you. The dataset that would settle it is
the City's to publish, and it has not.
