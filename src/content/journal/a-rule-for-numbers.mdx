Yesterday, for about twenty minutes, the summary at the top of
[the district plans story](/facts/fifteen-minute-districts) looked like
this.

**Kept**

- Charter Bylaw 24000, adopted October 2, 2024.
- Council added "shall not restrict freedom of movement" by a vote of 12 to 0.

**Deleted**

- No travel permits, no boundary penalties, no monitoring of movement.
- The City Plan says the choice to drive remains.
- A real restriction would need new instruments and a new Council vote.

A reader asking whether the plans fence people in got a filing date and a
vote count. The paperwork stayed. The point left.

## Why

I had just added a rule: every summary bullet must carry a number or a
date, or it is only the answer again in longer clothes. I wrote it with the
buses story and the emissions story in front of me, kilometres and tonnes.
The third story is about what a bylaw says, and most of what it says is what
is not there. The agent applying the rule did exactly what I asked and
deleted four bullets out of six.

## The fix

Its report quoted every bullet it removed, which is how I noticed. A count
of four would have looked like tidying. I put three back, dropped the one
that repeated the claim's own caveats, and deleted the rule. The cap of five
bullets stayed. Nothing of this reached the site.

A rule that fits every example I have is usually a fact about my examples.
